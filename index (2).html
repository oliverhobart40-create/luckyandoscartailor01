Before you order

Six questions worth asking

Good communication early on can help avoid rushed decisions and disappointing fit.

✂

How many fittings?

Ask what is included. Two to three fittings are common for a first suit, but the exact process varies.

◷

What is the timeline?

Share your departure or wedding date at the start. Leave time for adjustments rather than planning around the earliest possible collection.

▧

Can I inspect the cloth?

Ask to compare swatches in natural light and check the fabric composition, weight and weave.

⌁

What does the fitting show?

A first fitting may look unfinished. Focus on balance, shoulder position and how the garment feels.

✓

What alterations are included?

Clarify how final adjustments are handled and whether help is available after collection.

☆

Are reviews recent?

Read recent detailed reviews, not just star ratings. Look for comments about communication, fit and aftercare.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Who is the best tailor in Bangkok?

There is no single best tailor for everyone. Compare fabric range, fitting process, communication, recent reviews and location, then choose the shop that matches your needs.

How many fittings does a custom suit need?

Two to three fittings are common for a first suit. A first fitting checks the shape, another refines details, and a final check confirms the fit. Ask each shop what its process includes.

How long does a custom suit take in Bangkok?

Timing varies by shop, garment and schedule. Some shops advertise quick turnarounds, but confirm the timeline directly and leave room for alterations if possible.

What fabric is best for Bangkok’s climate?

Lightweight wool, linen and suitable blends are useful starting points. Compare swatches and ask about lining, breathability and the way the cloth drapes.

Do I need an appointment?

Policies differ. An appointment can help you secure time for measurements or fittings, especially if you are visiting Bangkok for only a few days.
