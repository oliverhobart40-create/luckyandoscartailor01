How many days does a custom suit take?

For a first suit, four or five days gives you more breathing room for fittings than a two-day visit. Use this as a planning guide rather than a promise of delivery.

Day 1 · ConsultationDiscuss the occasion, choose fabric and lining, take measurements and agree on the deadline.
Day 2 · First fittingTry the garment while it is still adjustable. Check the shoulders, chest, waist and overall balance.
Day 3 · AdjustmentsDepending on the shop’s process, attend another fitting and check sleeve, collar and trouser length.
Day 4 or 5 · Final checkTry on the finished garment, check movement and confirm collection or shipping arrangements.

With only two days, ask whether a shirt, trousers or alterations are more realistic than a fully fitted suit. The fitting FAQ explains why fittings matter and what to ask your tailor.

Before you land in Bangkok

A little preparation can make the first appointment more productive. Try to do these things before your flight:

  • Message the tailor with your arrival and departure dates.
  • Book an appointment for the day after you arrive, if possible.
  • Save reference photos showing the cut, lapels, pockets and fit you prefer.
  • Decide whether the garment is for work, a wedding, a formal event or travel.
  • Bring or photograph the shirt and shoes you expect to wear with the suit.
  • Ask how many fittings are included and what happens if the garment is not ready on time.

What should you order when time is short?

Two to three days: shirts, trousers or alterations

Simpler items may be more practical when there is little time for a full suit fitting. Ask what is possible before choosing fabric.

Four to five days: one suit with planned fittings

Keep the design straightforward, choose available fabric and reserve time for at least one fitting plus a final check.

A week or more: more complex pieces

A three-piece suit, separate jacket and trousers, or overcoat may need extra decisions and adjustment time. Confirm the schedule first.

Getting to fittings around Bangkok

Several shops in this guide are in or near the Sukhumvit area, while others are in shopping centres or nearby districts. The BTS Skytrain can make travel between appointments easier. Thailand’s official government portal notes that the first train service can begin at around 5:15 a.m., depending on the line and station; check the current timetable before setting out.

Use the Thailand government transport information as a starting point, then confirm the route for your particular appointment. Try to group appointments by area and avoid booking a fitting too close to your flight or airport transfer.

Five tips for a short stay

  • Be honest about your deadline. Ask the tailor to tell you if the schedule is unrealistic.
  • Keep the fitting. Skipping it can leave little opportunity to correct the fit.
  • Check key details. Look at the shoulders, collar, sleeve length, lining and stitching before accepting the garment.
  • Ask about shipping. Confirm cost, delivery estimates, tracking and alteration options before relying on overseas delivery.
  • Keep your measurements. Ask how you can access them for future orders.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a custom suit take in Bangkok?

Some tailors may complete an order in two to five days, but the timeline varies. Three to five days is a safer planning window if you want fittings and time for adjustments.

What is the best tailor in Bangkok for tourists?

It depends on your budget, style, location and schedule. Compare the fitting process, fabric choices, communication and the shop’s ability to meet your departure date.

Can I get a suit made in 24 hours?

Some shops may advertise very fast service, but availability and fit can vary. Ask exactly what is included and consider a shirt or trousers if you cannot allow time for a suit fitting.

Should I book before I arrive?

Yes. Booking ahead helps you check the shop’s availability, discuss your travel dates and potentially reserve a fitting slot shortly after arrival.

Can a tailor ship my order home?

Some shops offer international shipping. Confirm the price, delivery estimate, tracking, customs considerations and how alterations will be handled before ordering.
